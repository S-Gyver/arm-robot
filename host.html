ESP32 Firmware Guide & Wiring (2 - 6 Servos)

ขาต่อ GPIO ของ Servo แต่ละรุ่น:
2 Servos:
• S1 (Base): GPIO 16
• S2 (Gripper): GPIO 17
3 Servos:
• S1 (Base): GPIO 16
• S2 (Arm): GPIO 17
• S3 (Gripper): GPIO 18
4 Servos (Standard):
• S1 (Base): GPIO 16 | S2 (Shoulder): GPIO 17
• S3 (Elbow): GPIO 18 | S4 (Gripper): GPIO 19
5-6 Servos:
• S1: 16 | S2: 17 | S3: 18 | S4: 19
• S5 (Wrist Pitch): GPIO 21 | S6 (Wrist Roll): GPIO 22
